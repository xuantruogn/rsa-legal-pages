RSA — Terms of Use

Local test edition · Updated 2 November 2026

Purpose and availability

RSA is an experimental, owner-operated local assistant for drafting, editing, reviewing and submitting social-media content. This local edition is not offered as a public web service and is not guaranteed to be continuously available.

Authorized accounts

You must own or be authorized to manage any social account connected to RSA. RSA uses the permissions granted by the applicable platform. Users can revoke access using provider account settings. TikTok and other providers may limit or reject API requests or publishing.

Review before publishing

Users must review the precise content, selected destination and privacy settings before authorizing submission. Posts and media are subject to provider policies, account controls and moderation. A successful API submission does not guarantee that a post is public or remains available.

Responsibility for content

Users are responsible for factual accuracy, ownership and rights to media, advertising disclosures, and compliance with applicable law and provider policies. AI-generated text, images and videos may be inaccurate and require human review.

Local files and service changes

The local installation depends on the user's computer, installed services and configured providers. The application can change during development. Information provided here describes the test build, not a production service-level guarantee.

Questions

Use the public issue tracker for non-sensitive document corrections. Never share personal data or credentials there.
