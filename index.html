RSA — local AI assistant

Social content drafting and publishing integration · Desktop/local test version

RSA is an owner-operated local application for researching, drafting, creating media, reviewing and submitting social posts using the connected social networks' authorized APIs. The app is currently in development; these pages describe its actual test configuration, not a public hosted service.

Access to a TikTok account requires the account owner's explicit authorization. Content is only submitted after a separate review and approval step, subject to TikTok's policies and permissions.
