RSA — Privacy Policy

Local test edition · Updated 2 November 2026

Information handled

RSA processes connected social-account identifiers, granted scopes, access and refresh tokens, drafts, user-selected media, publishing settings, publication state, and local task records when needed to perform user-requested operations.

Storage and safeguards

In the current local installation, these records are stored on the configured local system and database. OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using an installation-specific encryption key. Computer security, backups and access to that machine remain the user's responsibility.

When data goes to external providers

After the owner initiates or approves an operation, RSA may transmit the relevant account credentials, draft, media or task information to the authorized TikTok, Meta, OpenAI or other configured service. Each provider processes this information under its own terms and privacy rules. RSA does not automatically make locally saved drafts public.

Retention and user controls

Local drafts, media and logs may remain stored until removed through available application controls or administrator data management. Connected accounts can be disconnected in RSA; provider-side revocation can be completed in the respective platform's account settings. Backups must be managed separately by the local computer owner.

Public website data

This information site is hosted using GitHub Pages and does not itself connect to TikTok accounts or receive OAuth tokens. GitHub may collect normal access logs for hosting and security.

Scope and questions

This notice describes RSA's owner-operated local development version; it does not claim a public multi-user service. For non-sensitive questions, use the issue tracker. Do not post personal data or account secrets there.
